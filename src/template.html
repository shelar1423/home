Welcome

Explore the home floor by floor, or walk through it as if you were there.

  • ▶
    TourPress Tour and sit back. It walks you through every floor and explains each room.
  • 🏠
    OverviewChoose a floor to see it from above. Drag to turn the house and pinch or scroll to zoom. Tap a room for details.
  • 🚶
    Walk insideTap the floor where you want to go, or use the round joystick. Drag to look around. Doors open by themselves.
  • 🛋
    Furniture and labelsUse the buttons at the top to hide furniture and see the empty rooms, or to hide the room names.
  • 👤
    Person viewSee a person walking through the house, with the camera following behind. It moves the same way as Walk inside.
  • ↑
    StairsWalk onto the stairs, or press the orange button that appears near them, to change floors.
